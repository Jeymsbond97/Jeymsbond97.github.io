Last week I attended KICEF 2026 in Daegu with our company. Taking part as an exhibitor alongside my team and showing our robotic arm systems was not just a great opportunity; it was a strong reminder of where the world is heading.

Walking through the exhibition, the pace of innovation in Physical AI, embodied humanoid systems, quadruped robots and smart home ecosystems was both breathtaking and humbling. Seeing these complex technologies move from labs to production floors redefines what is possible in modern automation.

As an engineer, moments like this are an important reality check. The more we build, the more we realize how vast this field is. The frontier of AI-driven robotics is expanding rapidly, and there is still so much to learn and so many boundaries to push. I am inspired to take these insights back to our current and future engineering work.

Keep going.
