I have completed the Google Project Management Professional Certificate.

For the past four months I set aside time to learn the fundamentals of project management, and gained a deeper understanding of planning, Agile practices, risk management, stakeholder communication and project execution.

Working as an engineer, I have come to appreciate that successful projects need more than strong technical skills. Clear planning, effective communication, adaptability and collaboration are what turn ideas into reliable results.

The course added a valuable perspective to my technical background, and I believe this combination will support my long-term professional growth.
