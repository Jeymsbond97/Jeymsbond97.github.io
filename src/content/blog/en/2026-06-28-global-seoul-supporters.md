Yesterday's official Global Seoul Supporters 2026 orientation and launch ceremony brought together international residents from 26 countries, with diverse professions, experiences and perspectives on building a more inclusive Seoul. I am honoured to take part as a representative of Uzbekistan.

Over my years in Korea I have experienced both the opportunities and the challenges of adapting to a new environment. I am grateful for the chance to share those experiences, learn from people with different backgrounds, and contribute ideas that make life easier and more welcoming for international residents across Korea.

My sincere thanks to International World in Korea (IWIK) and the Seoul Metropolitan Government for this opportunity.
