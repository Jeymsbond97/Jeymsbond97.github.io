I finally crossed the finish line of my first official trail marathon, and I am genuinely proud of myself.

A few months ago, even running 5 km on a flat road felt challenging. Then one day I started running out of curiosity, and somewhere along the way that curiosity turned into a habit and, eventually, into something I truly enjoy.

I finished 26th out of 213 runners. Maybe it is not an extraordinary result for some, but it means a lot to me, because I had only been running for a short time, and this was not an ordinary road race. It was a trail marathon through mountains: endless climbs, rocky paths, steep descents and hundreds of stairs, on a rainy day.

The biggest victory was not my finishing position. It was realizing how much my endurance has improved. Every evening run and every kilometre of training was worth it, and it showed me once again that consistency really does pay off.

There are much bigger races ahead, and I will keep challenging myself. See you at the next starting line.
