I promised myself that one day I would finish a full marathon, and I finally kept that promise.

In short, it was very difficult. I was not fully ready yet, and I felt it during the run. As if that was not enough, I lost my way and life added an extra 4 km. But I did it.

I just wanted to prove to myself that I could, and I proved it. The time was not great, but it is good enough for me.

Maybe it is time to find a new challenge, something bigger that can push me again. This is not the end. Life keeps moving.
